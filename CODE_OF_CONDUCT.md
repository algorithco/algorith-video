# Code of Conduct

AlgorithVideo aims to be a respectful place to build and discuss software. Everyone participating in the project is expected to treat others with consideration, regardless of experience, identity, background, nationality, disability, or personal beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively. Explain disagreements and give feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Welcome newcomers, assume good intent when reasonable, and allow people to ask questions and learn.
- Respect other people's time, boundaries, privacy, and decisions about how they participate.
- Acknowledge mistakes, accept constructive feedback, and correct harmful behavior when asked.
- Use public or synthetic material in reports and examples. Obtain permission before sharing someone's personal information or private media.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized comments or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable. Neither are stalking, doxxing, impersonation, repeated unwanted contact, deliberate disruption, or encouraging others to engage in these behaviors.

Technical disagreement does not justify abusive language or targeting a contributor. Repeatedly ignoring a maintainer's reasonable request to stop disruptive behavior can also lead to moderation.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents AlgorithVideo or Algorithco in a project role. Conduct elsewhere may be considered when it directly affects the safety of participation in the project.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the Algorithco maintainer at [otashdev1@gmail.com](mailto:otashdev1@gmail.com). Do not post personal details or accusations in a public issue.

Include the relevant links or screenshots, a description of what happened, and any immediate participation concerns. Share only information needed to investigate. Reports made in good faith must not lead to retaliation.

Suspected software vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement and review

Maintainers will review reports fairly, limit access to sensitive information, and consider the behavior's severity, impact, and history. They may remove or edit content, give a private warning, restrict a thread, temporarily suspend participation, or permanently exclude a participant. Serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate removal.

A person whose conduct is being reviewed should not decide the outcome of their own case. When possible, a different maintainer will handle a concern involving a maintainer. Affected participants may request a review through the same private reporting channel and provide relevant new information.

Maintainers will explain a moderation decision to the people involved when appropriate, while protecting the privacy of reporters and other participants. No response-time guarantee is made; the project is maintained according to contributor availability.
